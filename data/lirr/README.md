# LIRR access network (Section 4.2), 2026 published fares, service-based capacity

This is the AM peak commuter market from Long Island census tracts to New York City. There are three operators: LIRR commuter rail (seats on train services), LOCAL feeder transit, and PARK station parking. There is also an operator-less auto alternative, on the station-access leg and as the outside option.

- **Network:** 897 nodes, 8,320 links (2,275 owned) and 6 destination segments.
- **Cells:** 29,455 available segment-link cells, of which 4,673 carry positive flow.
- **Ground truth:** generated by Algorithm 2′ at τ⁰, with the LIRR peak one-way fares effective 4 January 2026 applied as a two-part link tariff. Supply scales are α_S = 0.25 for LIRR and 4 for LOCAL/PARK.
- **Gross surplus at the administered fares:** 894,503 $/hr, using the generator's utility.

New files in this version:
- `operator_budgets.csv`: per operator (LIRR, LOCAL, PARK) the budget F_m, the idle cost c_idle, the supply scale alpha_S and the estimation group aS_group, read by `build_lirr.py`.
- `services.csv`: the link-service incidence H. There are 2,631 services: 481 LIRR routes (origin station × terminal), 3 single-link rail services, 97 parking lots (PARK) and 2,050 LOCAL links. Each row lists the service's links (`|`-separated) and its cap.
- `services_at_tau0.csv`: seats deployed per service at the ground truth (Y⁰). These are observed, like y⁰, per Assumption 3.
- `fare_projection.csv`: the rail link fares.
- `fare_additivity_residual.csv`: the tariff and its fit to the published table.
- `fare_path_check.csv`: the published and modelled fare for every rail path.
- `station_constants.csv` and `asc_calibration.json`: the station calibration.

Provenance is in the top-level README.md. Scripts expect to be run with this directory as the working directory.
